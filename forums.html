SEWER_WITCH anarch, sector 4 WORD SIGNED 23:48 CET
A word-bond is not a promise. Everyone says it as though it were, and then they are astonished, thirty years later, by what it does.

The Word is freely given, once, and unbreakable for the life of both. If you give it to your childe to anchor their regalia, you have told the universe exactly where to look when someone wants to know who holds your loyalty. You have not protected them. You have published a map with your own name on it.

The discipline we use instead is Obfuscation — the same working, but the name it binds is yours, not theirs. Your childe learns to be looked through. It is slower work, and it is the only work that survives a sweep. The formal notes are in the archive under disciplines_reference; read that one before you decide, not after.

I am not against bonds. I am against bonds given because they felt like love at the time.
