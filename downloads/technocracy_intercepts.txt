THE TECNOCRACIA: INTERCEPT SUMMARY
(The enemy that files reality's paperwork)

NOT THE SI. DO NOT CONFUSE THEM.

  The Second Inquisition hunts vampires with cameras and clergy.
  The Technocracy hunts EXCEPTIONS TO THE RULES with auditors.

STRUCTURE (per intercepts):
  - Descends from the Order of Reason (founded 1325), the first
    organization to argue that miracles are a filing error.
  - Conventions of record:
      Iteration X      — machines, and the machine of machines.
      New World Order  — the story department. Consensus enforcement.
      The Syndicate    — ownership. Reality follows the money.
      Progenitors      — biology as engineering. We are a rounding
                         error in their models.
      Void Engineers   — the border patrol of consensus.

DOCTRINE, PARAPHRASED FROM A GENEVA INTERCEPT:
  "Vampires are reality deviants. Deviants are inventory."

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE NETWORK:
  - They do not stake. They RECLASSIFY. A node that appears in a
    Technocracy census has already been deleted in principle; the
    deletion arrives on a schedule.
  - Their weapons are paradox audits: make the exception so
    improbable that consensus repossesses it.
  - The Camarilla survives them by being boring. Anarchs survive by
    being diffuse. We survive by being a rumor with good backups.

COUNTERMEASURE (network doctrine):
  Never answer a census. Never explain a miracle. If a terminal
  starts receiving beautifully formatted questionnaires, burn the
  node and file the burn under weather.
