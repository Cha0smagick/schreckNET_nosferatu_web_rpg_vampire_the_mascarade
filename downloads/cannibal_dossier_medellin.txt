SCHRECKNET VLA-7 // EXPEDIENTE: TAIL-FEEDING EVENTS
classification: nodes-only / annex relay
provenance: compilation of operator filings + street
 leeches, 2003-2025, unverified
=====================================================

SUMMARY. Mortal media in South and Central America has
 logged, for two decades, scattered reports of
 cannibalism: survival cases in the Medellin storm
 drains, ritual convictions in Guatemala, a Managua
 house that fed its own, body parts sold as food in
 three capital cities. Kindred analysis is unanimous
 and boring: most of it is mortals being mortals.
 The rest is us.

STANDING DIRECTIVE: do not investigate feeding grounds
 from the terminal. A feeding ground maps its
 investigators. File, tag, and walk away.

-----------------------------------------------------
FILE A — MEDELLIN / THE DRAIN LINE
Comuna 13 and the Gran Afluente. Storm-drain survival
 cannibalism made mortal news in the 2010s; Kindred
 read it as cover. A Nosferatu line (the Drain Line)
 has hunted in those tunnels since the 90s and lets
 mortal cases soak the blame. Flag: DRAIN-TAIL
 (reported mortal cases cluster where Kindred feeding
 pauses — a pause is a lease renewal). The Line
 sells the tunnels' goodwill back to the Court of
 Tenochtitlan's trade partners. Do not enter the
 Gran Afluente unescorted. Escorted is worse.

FILE B — BOGOTA / THE RECOLETA PANTRY
A ghoul pantry operating out of funeral workers'
 housing: bodies pre-dressed as burial stock, drained
 portion by portion, re-stitched, buried on schedule.
 Mortal press calls it body-selling. It is catering.
 Flag: PANTRY-ORDER (orders precede deaths by 6
 days). Hecata denies it loudly, which is how you
 know the fee goes to Venice.

FILE C — CARACAS / COMMUNAL PLATES
Sabbat pack practice dressed as gang atrocity. The
 city's collapse made every horror plausible, which
 is the point. Flag: PLATE-SWAP (news photos of
 "gang" scenes reused across years with new
 captions — someone edits coverage, not evidence).

FILE D — GUATEMALA CITY / THE SINK CULT
Mortal accomplices of the Sink (see red room plate
 04). Recruitment ritual involves a shared meal in
 the dark. Two convictions on record; both convicts
 recanted, then re-confessed, then stopped speaking.
 Flag: DARK-MEAL (recruits keep the napkin).

FILE E — MANAGUA / THE HOUSE THAT FED
A family mortuary house that practiced "family
 nutrition" across two generations before exposure.
 Kindred interest is archaeological: the founder was
 a failed Embrace who never stopped being hungry.
 His line, if it exists, files under nagaraja
 sighting reports flagged UNVERIFIED.

FILE F — SAN SALVADOR / THE COLD BUFFET
Body parts recovered from a dead mall food court
 (see red room plate 06). Mortal case collapsed for
 lack of a suspect. The food court reopens for
 private events. Flag: BUFFET-SCHEDULE (reservations
 posted on nothing; the mall's security lights turn
 off in a pattern, not at random).

-----------------------------------------------------
ASSESSMENT. The pattern is not appetite. It is
 logistics: each site is a feeding cover that mortals
 supply voluntarily, through crime, desperation, or
 cult. The Kindred role is bookkeeping. VLA-7 files
 this expedient as a logistics map. Handle it as one.
