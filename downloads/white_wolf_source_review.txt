SECTION 1 — CAMARILLA OFFICES (the ladder above the prince)
------------------------------------------------------------
prince        - domain ruler, holds Tradition rights (Creation,
                Final Death "up to a point").
seneschal     - the left hand. Runs the city when the prince
                cannot be seen running it.
keeper of
elysium       - maintains neutral ground. Breaches there are
                punished twice.
sheriff       - prince's enforcement arm; may demand any
                Kindred present for questioning.
scourge       - hunts the UNWELCOME. In hard domains that means
                Caitiff and thin-bloods on sight.
harpy         - records boons and slights. Social credit office.
                Never insult a harpy twice.
primogen      - elder council. Advisory on paper, co-rulers in
                older cities.
archons       - sect-level agents dispatched to domains in
                crisis (Sabbat war, Masquerade collapse, elder
                feuds). They outrank every prince but answer to
                the justicars.
justicars     - seven, one per founding clan. Outermost law.
inner circle  - the actual top. It meets; nobody admits it meets.
alastors      - specialized archons. See Section 2.

flag OFFICE-CREEP: if a name in a domain holds an office no
charter lists, log the office. Sometimes the office is the
signal, not the person.

SECTION 2 — THE RED LIST (ANATHEMA)
------------------------------------------------------------
- The Inner Circle maintains a list of Kindred it wants dead on
  sight: the Red List. Members are called Anathema.
- Procedure per source: TWO justicars must agree to prosecute,
  the name goes before the Inner Circle, all assembled
  representatives must concur. That is a high bar. The list is
  short. That is why it is feared.
- Hunting is done by ALASTORS — archons sworn to the list.
- Killing an Anathema grants a "Trophy" — a major boon, worth
  more than most unlife savings.
- NODE APPLICATION: cross-check every high-threat registry file
  against Red List rumors. If one of OUR files is on THEIR list,
  we have leverage; if one of our COOPERATORS is, we have a
  problem inside a month.

directive: do not volunteer Red List status to anyone. Boons
attract hunters, and hunters bring Second Inquisition dogs.

SECTION 3 — THE INCONNU (monks of the long game)

  bonds, "beyond the game."

  Carpathians. Below them, MONITORS — watchers assigned to
  observe whole supernatural institutions (the Camarilla, the
  Sabbat, mage chantries, and — we assume — networks like this
  one). Traveling agents are called EQUITES.

  Danika Ruthven (Tzimisce, 6th, Council),
  Demetrius (Malkavian, 6th, Council).

SECTION 4 — ANTEDILUVIAN APOCRYPHA (new fragments only)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Bracket convention: scholars write [Ventrue], [Toreador] to
  name the founders without saying their names, "as a
  precaution against drawing their ire." Adopt the convention
  in all files. It costs nothing and the alternative costs
  everything.
- Third Generation capacity per old rulebooks: traits to 10,
  unknown blood pool, unknown spend per turn. Practically:
  if a fight looks winnable, you have misidentified the enemy.
- The GEHENNA sourcebook scenarios introduce previously
  unknown founders, each scenario a different set. One is a
  woman called THE SHAPER who took the name "Sarah"; unique
  Discipline moves and reshapes matter itself. Treat as
  scenario-lore: possibly canon, possibly a lesson dressed as
  a story.
- Setite heresy: Set Embraced HIMSELF; the other twelve
  Antediluvians were made to thwart him.
- Baali priest ANGRA MAINYU claimed visions that CAINE HAD
  FIVE CHILDREN, not three. If true, two clans are missing
  from every registry on earth.
- Path of Lilith variant: ARIKEL and MALKAV were twins,
  children of Lilith, with a younger sister NAHEMA who took
  the Embrace and descended to Hell without losing touch —
  her spirit bound to her brothers and ALL their descendants.

flag SISTER-CLAUSE: any contacts who speak of "the sister" in
Toreador or Malkavian business are either lunatics, Liars, or
correct. All three are dangerous. Log wording verbatim.

SECTION 5 — MASQUERADE MECHANICS (how breaches actually die)
------------------------------------------------------------
- A breach is any evidence of vampiric nature: feeding, visible
  Discipline use, wounds knitting in public. Ghouls count as
  standing potential breaches.
- Enforcement is self-policing with penalties, and princes
  famously prefer "an early morning dusting over a thorough
  search" — the cleanup is often assigned by proximity, not
  guilt.
- The VENTRUE treat Masquerade-patching as a science: cash,
  Dominate, media surgery, whatever the patch needs.
- Origin note: Rafael de Corazon's speech of 1450 made
  enforcement the Camarilla's founding policy.

NODE APPLICATION: our own site-breach protocol mirrors this.
You are the dusting when it is small and the witness when it
is not.
