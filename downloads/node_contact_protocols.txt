FILE: NODE CONTACT PROTOCOLS
how to reach a name in the registry without becoming a name
in someone else's registry.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 0 — YOU DO NOT CONTACT. YOU ARRANGE CIRCUMSTANCES.
------------------------------------------------------------
Kindred do not take calls. They notice patterns. The only
safe introduction is one where the contact concludes you were
ALREADY in their world — a courier they almost hired, a rat
line they almost used, a correction to a chart they trust.
If a first contact reads as a request, close the channel.

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------
- PIRATE RADIO / BROADCAST: Sadie LaRue (New Orleans,
  SIGNAL-30). Leave requests inside song requests read
  on-air two nights later. Fee history: corrections, not
  favors. Do not dedicate a song. She reads dedications as
  debts.
- THE WIRE ITSELF: Mitnick (Los Angeles). Route a data
  artifact where it will be found — never attach a note.
  Receipt arrives as a one-line file you did not send
  yourself.
- OPEN-LEDGER COOPERATORS: Bram Verhoeven (Rotterdam) and
  Padre Huesos (Lima) work WITH this node. Protocol: written
  queries only, one question per message, no follow-ups
  faster than nine days. They cooperate because we are
  quiet. Loudness ends cooperation.
- THE SILENT BARTERS: Octavia Rehn (Vienna) answers in
  silence — count what she does NOT sing about. The Lender
  of Last Resorts (ROOM-12) answers in paperwork: request
  forms appear pre-filled where you last filed your own.
  Never sign what ROOM-12 returns. Review it in a mirror
  first; if the mirror lags, burn it.
- COURT FIGURES (princes, primogen, harpies): approach via
  boon-broker only, and only when YOU hold the boon. A
  court visit you did not pay for is a summons.
- ANTIQUARIANS AND ARCHIVISTS (Ankla, al-Ashrad, the
  Sommer-cache dealers): never open with a request for the
  item. Open with a correction to something they already
  published. Scholars forgive fees; they do not forgive
  sloppiness.
- THE META FILES (The Census Taker, The Blind Scale, The
  Encore, The Unasked Question): there is no protocol.
  There is a survey. If one approaches you, you are already
  the data. File everything, answer nothing, move.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 2 — FEES (what things actually cost)
------------------------------------------------------------
Standard currency, in rising order of price:
  a correction > a silence > a name of the dead > a map
  that does not exist yet > a first cry > vitae > your
  standing to accuse > your name in someone else's ledger.
Never pay vitae to an information broker. It converts a
client into a holding.
Fees are quoted ONCE. Re-negotiating a fee re-opens the
contract at the other party's choice of terms.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 3 — TIMING
------------------------------------------------------------
First response window on this network: 3 to 9 nights.
Faster than 3 = a copy of them, or a trap.
Slower than 9 = the message was read by someone who is not
them. Log the delay; delays are signatures.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 4 — DENIABILITY LADDER
------------------------------------------------------------
Every channel has three grades:
  grade 1 (burnable): dead drops, song requests, public
    corrections. Use freely, assume read.
  grade 2 (costly): intermediaries, ghouls, boon-brokers.
    Each use is a name given away. Spend like ammunition.
  grade 3 (unrecoverable): direct meeting. Only for
    contracts you would stake your name on, and only on
    ground the OTHER party does not control.

------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 5 — WHEN A CONTACT REACHES FOR YOU
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Verify the approach against the registry. Unlisted names
   offering listed knowledge = two problems, not one.
2. Reply only with information you are prepared to lose.
3. Never confirm your node, your city, or your transmits —
   even to confirm them false.
4. If the approach cites a debt you do not remember owing,
   the debt is real and already old (see Ventrue antitribu
   file). Report to VLA-7 before sundown.

------------------------------------------------------------
STANDING DIRECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
The registry tells you who exists. These protocols tell you
how to knock. Every knock is logged on both sides of the
door. Knock accordingly.
