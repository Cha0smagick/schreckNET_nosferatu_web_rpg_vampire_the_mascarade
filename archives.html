SERVER NOTICE

Downloads are free. There is no account, no key, no handshake, and nothing that runs when you open a file. That is unusual enough that people assume it is a trick. It is not a trick. It is a mirror that has given up on protecting itself and would rather be useful.

Burn what you read. Not because the files are dangerous — they are text — but because this network survives on nobody holding a complete copy of anything, including us.
