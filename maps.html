CARTOGRAPHY

Two charts. The first is a warren — a hole in the ground, drawn to scale because a warren is the only place on this network where the map is the territory. The second is the mesh itself, drawn as relative position only, because the moment a relay can be located it stops being a relay.

HANDLING — THIS PLATE IS A LIST OF ROOMS

A warren plate is the single most dangerous document this network produces. It is not forbidden, because a warren nobody can draw is a warren nobody can reach when the sweeps come. It is restricted, and it leaves this page only as a burned impression, and the burned copy is never current. If you are reading a copy of this sheet, assume the copy is older than the tunnels.

Chambers F and K were re-surveyed after the sweep. Chamber F is no longer a chamber. The mark remains because removing a mark is how you lie to yourself about a hole.

PLATE II — RELAY TOPOLOGY · RELATIVE POSITION ONLY · NOT AN ADDRESS LIST

Twelve tracked positions. Positions are drawn where the cities are; the lines are drawn where trust is. A solid line means two relays have exchanged a handshake within the hour. Where the burned node sat, the line is struck through, because the network has not yet agreed to pretend it was never there.

WHY THE LINES CROSS THE ATLANTIC FOURTEEN TIMES

Because we built the mesh to survive the loss of a continent, and then lost a city instead. The Vienna burn severed the eastern spine; the reconstruction runs the traffic the long way round, and every long way round is a place a hunter learns to watch. The graph is ugly because the alternative was a graph that ends in an ash box in the Operngasse.
